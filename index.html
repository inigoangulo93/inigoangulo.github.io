How I think

From ambiguity

to measurable outcome.

five steps · each kills one layer of uncertainty · hover to watch each one think

01

Listen

The real problem is rarely the brief.

Briefs describe symptoms. Behavior describes problems. Session data, recordings and shadowing come before any sketch, and every stated requirement is a hypothesis until it survives contact with what people actually do.

02

Frame

Every problem becomes one decision.

A problem framed as a decision has a finite answer. What must this system produce, who acts on it, and when? Every feature either serves that decision or gets cut, and most of the value is in the cutting.

03

Architect

Structure first. Screens last.

Information architecture, decision flows and component logic come before any interface. When the structure is right, the screens almost draw themselves. When it's wrong, no amount of visual polish will save them.

04
A
control
B
earned it

Prove

Every design ships with its measurement plan.

Opinion starts the work; instrumented outcomes finish it. Every design ships knowing how it will be measured, and a result that can't be verified is a result that doesn't exist yet.

05

Close the loop

The signal after release beats the plan before it.

Shipping is not a handoff. Reality votes after release, and systems get tuned in production: reading the signal, adjusting, and letting what actually happens outrank what was planned.

// about me

Inigo Angulo

PRODUCT STRATEGY · PRODUCT MANAGEMENT · DESIGN

I’m a product builder based in Zurich, 9+ years across commerce, fintech, PropTech, and travel. I operate across the full stack of product work: strategy, design, and the analytics that prove it.

I work on complex, data-rich products where clarity, speed, and business impact matter. My focus is turning messy systems into products people trust and decisions the business can act on — from problem definition and prioritisation to shipped interface.

Recent work spans product and experience strategy for AI-enabled commerce platforms at WPP, the checkout experimentation programme at GetYourGuide (10 A/B tests, +11% conversion), product leadership for a luxury trading platform at Crowdhouse AG, and ING Bank’s multi-country design system.

My work sits at the intersection of product strategy, behavioural analytics, and design execution. I use PostHog, GA4, and Mixpanel not just to validate decisions, but to find the friction, blind spots, and opportunities that define what to build next.

Right now I’m going deep on agentic product patterns: how humans supervise AI systems that do real work. The flagship case study on this site is the most complete expression of that so far.

// beyond work

Outside of work, I’m deeply drawn to European medieval history, especially the symbolic weight, visual language, and cultural continuity of the period. I have a particular fascination with Gothic typography and the way form can carry identity, authority, and atmosphere across centuries.

Art keeps my sense of composition and balance alive. Photography helps me slow down and notice what most people miss. Hiking through Swiss trails is how I reset, think clearly, and usually where new ideas begin to take shape.
