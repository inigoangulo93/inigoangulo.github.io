01 Problem

Every empty room is burning money

Enso's coliving model operates on occupancy. When a habi (individual living unit) sits empty past its target move-in date, the company absorbs rent, utilities, and maintenance with zero revenue. In Q3 alone, 23% of rooms exceeded their target date.

The core issue: our listing page treated every room equally. A high-risk vacancy that needed to fill this week displayed the same card as a room available three months out. No urgency differentiation. No conversion pressure calibrated to actual business risk.

The hypothesis: if we could surface the right urgency signals at the right time, we could accelerate bookings for high-risk inventory without degrading trust.

02 Approach

Design principles for ethical urgency

Urgency design is a minefield. Booking.com-style dark patterns erode trust. We needed a framework that drove action while remaining honest. Three principles governed every decision:

Design rationaleProgressive disclosure means: start clean. Only add elements when the business risk justifies them. Level 1 rooms look minimal. Level 4 rooms carry the full weight of urgency signals, deals, and perks. Every signal is factual. Viewer counts are live sessions, not fabricated. Discounts are real price reductions against the original, not anchor pricing tricks. "Last room available" means literally one room left. The user decides. We surface context. We never manufacture panic.
03 Framework

Risk-calibrated card system

Five risk tiers drive the progressive addition of urgency elements. Each tier maps to days remaining before a room's vacancy becomes a financial liability. The server calculates risk level per room and surfaces the appropriate card variant.

08 Funnel analysis

Where the uplift compounded

Card click-through was the primary lever (+45.8%), but the gains compounded downstream: room view rate +11.7%, checkout start +12.9%.

09 Segmentation

Breakdown by city, device, and risk level

Mobile showed the highest uplift (+50%) because urgency signals have outsized impact on small screens. And Level 4 drove +86% uplift, confirming that stacking perks at high-risk tiers materially accelerates conversion.

12 Raw thinking

Process notes

Risk model logicThe system calculates risk per room, not per property. Server-side calculation. No client-side logic exposed. Risk level updates daily via cron job against booking pipeline data.
Why "scarcity, not pressure"Early explorations had countdown timers. Internal testing: 4/5 said "this feels like Booking.com." Enso's brand is premium coliving. Trust > urgency. Always. The pivot: show what's real instead of manufacturing time pressure. Same conversion lift, no brand damage.
What I'd do differently1. Start with mobile-only. 73% of traffic is mobile. 2. Track cross-device journeys from Day 1. 3. A/B test individual signal elements, not the full bundle.
