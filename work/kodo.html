02 — The v1 Failure

A beautiful dashboard I couldn't trust with my own money

v1 had 23 visible metrics, 4 charts, real-time P&L, and order book depth. It looked like a professional trading terminal. Then I deposited real money and turned it on.

Within 30 minutes: I couldn't tell at a glance whether I was okay. The information was all there — spread across panels, charts, numbers. But under the stress of actual financial risk, I couldn't synthesize it fast enough.

v2 was rebuilt around one question: "Am I okay right now?"
