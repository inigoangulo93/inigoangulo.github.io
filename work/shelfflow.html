Enterprise Commerce / AI Workflow Systems / Decision Architecture

The operating system for commerce content at scale. One brief in, retailer-ready content out, with humans owning every decision that ships.

ShelfFlow is a structured workflow platform for enterprise commerce teams. It coordinates AI-assisted content generation across SKUs, retailers, and markets, while keeping human authority over every approval that reaches the shelf. I led product design end-to-end: workflow architecture, state machine design, decision authority mapping, AI orchestration model, and the full operator interface.
