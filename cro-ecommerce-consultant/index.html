The leaks I find most often

01

The step you are not measuring

Most funnels are instrumented at the stage level. The drop is between stages: the back button, the tab switch, the return visit with no memory of the cart.

02

Trust gaps, not payment problems

Shipping, returns, price surprises and reassurance at the moment of commitment convert more than another payment method.

03

Catalogue and search friction

Filters that hide inventory, product pages that answer the wrong questions, search that returns the catalogue instead of the answer.

How a CRO engagement runs

  1. AuditTwo weeks: analytics, session recordings, support tickets and a walk through your funnel on a real phone. Output: the ranked list of leaks with the evidence for each.
  2. PlanExperiments ordered by expected impact and cost, with success criteria written before anything is built.
  3. BuildI design the fix and work with your engineers, or with AI agents if you have no bandwidth, so every test ships A/B ready.
  4. ReadStatistical reads with sample sizes agreed in advance. No peeking, no calling winners at 80% confidence.
  5. CompoundThe wins go into a playbook your team keeps running after I leave.

Questions I get asked

How is CRO consulting priced?

A fixed price for the audit, then a monthly retainer for the experiment programme, usually three to six months. Numbers after a first call and a look at your traffic.

Do you need access to our analytics?

Yes. GA4 or equivalent, session recordings if you have them, and read access to the experiment tool. Without data it is opinion, and you can get opinion for free.

What if we do not have an A/B testing tool?

I set one up. For most stacks that is a day of work, and for low traffic sites I will tell you when sequential tests are a better idea than a tool.

Can AI agents build the test variants?

Yes, that is how I work on my own product. For a CRO programme it means variants ship in days, not sprints, and your engineers review instead of build.

Which markets do you know?

Mainly Europe: Switzerland, Germany, Spain, Portugal, UK, with multi market checkout experience from GetYourGuide and Enso.

30 minutes, no deck. I will tell you if I am the right person and, if not, who might be.
