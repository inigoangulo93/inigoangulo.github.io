What I get called for

01

A flow that leaks users

Checkout, onboarding, sign up, KYC. I map where people drop, why, and redesign the step with a measurable target, not a prettier screen.

02

A product that grew without a system

Twelve shades of the same button, three date pickers. I build the design system from the tokens up, and I make sure engineering adopts it, which is the hard part.

03

Complex data, no clear interface

Dashboards, admin panels, operational tools. Products where the user is on a deadline and ambiguity costs money.

How I work with design and engineering

  1. Research with a questionNot a persona exercise. Interviews, session recordings and analytics aimed at one decision we need to make.
  2. Flows before screensThe state machine of the product first: every path, every error, every empty state. Screens come after, and they are faster.
  3. Figma that mirrors codeComponents, variables and tokens named like the codebase. Engineers get a spec, not a picture.
  4. Measured hand offEach change ships with the metric it should move and how we will read it.
  5. The library stays aliveI leave a documented system and a routine for keeping it in sync with production.

Questions I get asked

Fractional, contract or project?

All three. Fractional (two to three days a week), a fixed contract for a redesign, or a scoped project like a design system. Pricing follows the format; I quote after a first call.

Do you do research as well as UI?

Yes. Most of what makes a redesign work is upstream of the interface. I run interviews and read analytics before I open Figma.

Can you work with our existing design system?

Yes, and I prefer it. I audit what exists, fix what blocks the team, and extend rather than replace.

Do you code?

I ship production software on my own product with AI agent teams, so I know what a Figma file costs to build. With your team I design and hand off; I do not replace your engineers.

Where are you based?

Near Zurich, Switzerland. Hybrid for Swiss teams, remote across Europe.

Tell me what you are trying to ship.

30 minutes, no deck. I will tell you if I am the right person and, if not, who might be.
