Build / 4 min / Agentic workflows

Hold the Gate

Six agents can do the work. Who is allowed to say yes?

From ShelfFlow, a content pipeline for an enterprise CPG team. Set the scope, build the pipeline on the canvas, give its flagged branch a gate and an owner, run the launch and take the last gate yourself. Then compare your pipeline with the one that shipped.

1 launch, up to 6 choicesAbout 4 minutesReal rulesSynthetic data
About the data

The rules are the ones the product enforces: an agent cannot reach Publish without a gate, a gate cannot run without a named owner, and the batch shortcut stays locked until each choice has been opened. The launch, the products and the copy are synthetic, and the client is under NDA.

Best with a mouse or trackpad: blocks are dragged onto the line and sockets are pulled to connect. The design behind it is in the ShelfFlow case study.
