The full log: all six calls, results and reasons
  1. EXP-01Hold-your-spot countdown: "We'll hold your spot for 30:00"+3.1% payment completion; Urgency related support tickets: zeroSHIPPED Urgency reframed as service: we reserve for the user, not against them. The top single win of the programme.
  2. EXP-06Price-expiry countdown: "Book now, the price rises when the timer ends"+4.2% click through to payment; −9 pts trust survey; Refund intent proxy: upKILLED A win that spends trust is a loss with a delay. One timer serves the user, this one threatens them. Killed on principle and on data.
  3. EXP-07Promo code as a quiet link instead of an open field−31% exits to hunt for coupons; Net checkout completion: upSHIPPED An empty field is an invitation to leave and go coupon hunting. A link keeps the option without issuing the invitation. Shipped.
  4. EXP-08Single-page checkout: collapse every step into one view+0.4% (n.s.) desktop conversion; −0.9% mobile conversion; Render time, mid range Android: about +1.2 sKILLED 72% of the programme's uplift lived on mobile. You do not trade your best segment for elegance. The principle survived the kill: unbroken context, shipped as two named steps (EXP-02).
  5. EXP-09Email-first continue, account creation deferredflat (n.s.) new user conversion, v1; Session recordings: still hesitating at "create account"ITERATED Flat is not dead when the qualitative signal survives. v2 paired email first with SSO and moved account creation after purchase: +1.1%, shipped.
  6. EXP-10Reorder payment methods to match each market+1.7% 2 of 4 markets; flat the other 2 marketsITERATED Not a global truth, a local one. It became a per market rollout with a shared default, instead of forcing one answer on four markets.
