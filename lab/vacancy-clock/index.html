About the data

The five tiers and which signals each one activates are the system that shipped at ENSO Co-Living across Lisbon, Valencia, Madrid and Barcelona. Rooms, prices and viewer counts here are illustrative. One honest note: in production the viewer count was a pressure element, not live data; every other signal reflected the room’s real situation. The daily cost is the average of about €4.2K a month per empty room, spread over 30 days.
